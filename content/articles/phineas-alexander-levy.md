I married Phineas (Phin) Alexander Levy in October 1953 at [Brixton Synagogue](https://www.jewishgen.org/jcr-uk/london/brixton/index.htm) which is where Phin was a member and his father was a warden.
Phin grew up in London, England and was British.

![Wedding 1953](/images/phineas-rachel-wedding.jpg)

We dated after meeting in South London.
Phin was a patient man when it came to dating me.
After we were married we moved and lived in Copenhagen, Denmark and returned a few years later.

His parents (Solomon and Linda Levy) wanted him to become a solicitor (like his father); however, Phin became a printing engineer focused on newspaper printing presses.
Phin worked on many printing presses in London, including the The London Times, The Financial Times, and other newspapers.
In the US, he worked on USA Today's newspaper presses.
Additionally, Phin worked on presses around Europe, Cyprus, India, and for a nearly two years, a newspaper in Kenya.

In the early 60's, we moved to Bromley Kent and joined the [Catford and Bromley Synagogue](https://catfordsynagogue.org.uk/), our local Jewish shul.
In the late 60's, Phin helped with the new design and rebuilding of the much-expanded Synagogue.
That work was complete in 1968 and our son Martin was bar mitzvahed in 1970 at that new shul.

We had two children. Martin, who now lives in America, and Shelley who lives in South London.

Phin passed away, after 52 years of marriage, at the age of 75.
